# Why our app explains instead of giving answers

If you ask our app for the final answer to a homework problem, you'll usually get an explanation of the concept and the method instead. That's on purpose. Our app is a study partner, so it's built to help you learn the material, not to finish your assignment for you.

## What you'll get instead

Ask about a problem and our app will walk through the idea behind it and the steps for working it out, so you can do the next one yourself. You can keep asking follow-up questions in the same chat to dig into a step that feels confusing.

Some ways to get more out of it:

- Say what you've already tried and where you got stuck.
- Ask for the explanation in different words if the first one doesn't click.
- Ask for a similar example to practice with.
- Upload your notes or assignment as context. See [Upload class materials](../study-tools/upload-class-materials).

## Check your understanding

Once a topic makes sense, you can generate study materials from the chat, like flashcards or practice questions, to test yourself. See [Generate and view study materials](../study-tools/generate-and-view-study-materials).

If something about our app isn't working the way you expect, [contact our support team](https://studywise.adarshrkumar.app/support).
