# Choose a subject and mode

On the home page, we group study modes by subject. Pick the subject and mode that best fit your question to start a focused chat. For example, choose Math, then Algebra for algebra questions, or Science, then Biology for biology.

## Find a mode

You can browse the subject groups on the home page, or open the subject search and type a subject or keyword. Search checks mode names, descriptions, and subject names. If there are no matches, try a broader term or clear the search to see all modes again.

Choose **General** when your question is broad or doesn't fit one of the listed modes. For a question that fits a particular class, a more specific mode helps keep the conversation focused.

## Start a different topic

Each chat is a separate conversation. To start another one, return to the home page and choose a mode again. Your existing chats are available from your profile under **My Chats**.

Once you choose a mode, ask your question in the chat. You can keep the conversation focused by choosing a mode that matches the subject, or pick **General** when you're not sure which one fits.
