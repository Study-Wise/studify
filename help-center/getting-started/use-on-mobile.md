# Use our app on your phone

We don't have a native iPhone or Android app yet. You can still use our app on your phone in your browser, and you can install it to your home screen as a progressive web app (PWA) so it opens like an app.

## Open it in your browser

Go to our website in your phone's browser and sign in the same way you would on a computer. Your chats and study materials are tied to your account, so they're there on any device.

Our app isn't fully designed for small screens yet, so some pages may feel tighter on a phone than on a laptop. A mobile-friendly layout is something we plan to add, but we can't say when.

## Install it to your home screen

**On iPhone or iPad (Safari):**

1. Open our app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

**On Android (Chrome):**

1. Open our app in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**, then confirm.

Once it's installed, tap the Studify icon on your home screen to open our app in its own window, without the browser bar. You still need an internet connection, and it works the same as the website.

If the install option doesn't show up, try a different browser or refresh the page and look again. If you're still stuck, [contact our support team](https://studywise.adarshrkumar.app/support) and tell us your phone and browser.
