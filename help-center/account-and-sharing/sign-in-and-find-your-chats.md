# Sign in and find your chats

Sign in to keep your chats saved to your account and to return to them later. Our app may take you to the sign-in page when you choose a study mode or open a private chat.

## Sign in or create an account

Sign in with your email and password, or choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**. If you don't have an account yet, choose the sign-up link and follow the prompts to create one.

If you're redirected to sign in while opening a chat, sign in and try again. The chat link may have a return address, so you can continue to the page you originally wanted.

## Find a saved chat

1. Sign in to the account you used for the chat.
2. Open your profile from the site navigation.
3. Find **My Chats** and choose the chat you want to reopen.

Chats belong to the account that created them. If a chat is missing, check that you're signed in with the right account. Your profile also shows the name and email address associated with the account.

If you still can't sign in or find a chat, [get in touch with our support team](https://studywise.adarshrkumar.app/support). Tell us what happened and which account email you used. Never send your password.
