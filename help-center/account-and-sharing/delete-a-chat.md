# Delete a chat

You can delete a chat you no longer need. Deleting removes the chat from your account, and only the chat owner can do it.

## Remove a chat from your account

1. Open a chat that you own. The delete button is in the left-hand sidebar under **Share Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete Chat**.
3. Check the chat name in the confirmation box, then choose **Delete**.

Once the chat is deleted, you'll be taken to a new chat in the same mode.

## Before you delete

Deleting a chat can't be undone. The conversation, its generated study materials, and its uploaded files are all removed, and a public share link will stop working. If you only want to stop sharing the chat, switch **Thread Visibility** back to **Private** in **Share Settings** instead. To remove just one uploaded file, use the delete control next to its name in **Uploaded Files**.

If the chat doesn't delete, refresh the page and try again. If you still need a hand, [contact our support team](https://studywise.adarshrkumar.app/support).
