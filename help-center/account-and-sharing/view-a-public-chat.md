# View a public chat

If someone shares a chat with you, you can open it with its link and read the conversation and its study materials. Signed-in users can view public chats, and the chat stays under its owner's account.

## Open a shared chat

1. Sign in to your account. You need to be signed in to view a public chat.
2. Open the full link you were sent. If you were redirected to sign in, sign in and the link should take you back to the chat.
3. Read the conversation, then use the right-hand sidebar to open any study materials in it.

## What you can do in a public chat

You can view the chat and its study materials. Only the owner can generate or regenerate materials, upload files, change **Share Settings**, or delete the chat. If you want to study the same topic yourself, choose a subject and mode on the home page to start your own chat.

The owner can switch a chat back to **Private** at any time, and then its link will no longer open for anyone else. If a link won't open, see [A chat or page will not open](../troubleshooting/a-chat-or-page-will-not-open). If you still need a hand, [contact our support team](https://studywise.adarshrkumar.app/support).
